# Content & Copy Guidelines: dot-moments

## Core Tone

- **Warm & Encouraging**: Use as if speaking to a close friend
- **Casual**: No corporate-speak, no jargon
- **Gentle**: Never judgmental or pressuring
- **Curious**: Invite reflection without demanding answers
- **Brief**: Short, scannable text. One sentence per idea.

---

## Core Messaging Principles

### 1. Celebrate Starting, Not Finishing

✅ **Good**:
- "시작했다는 것만 기록해요." (We only record that you started.)
- "완료하지 않아도 괜찮아요." (It's okay if you don't finish.)
- "점 하나가 찍혀요." (A dot gets placed.)

❌ **Avoid**:
- "과제 완료" (Task completed)
- "목표 달성" (Goal achieved)
- "성공했어요" (Success!)

### 2. Reduce Perfectionism Language

✅ **Good**:
- "부담 없이 툭 적어요." (Jot it down casually.)
- "완벽하지 않아도 괜찮아요." (Imperfection is okay.)
- "잘 쓰지 않아도 돼요." (You don't have to write well.)

❌ **Avoid**:
- "정확히 적으세요." (Write precisely.)
- "완벽하게 작성하세요." (Write perfectly.)
- "꼭 완료하셔야 합니다." (You must complete it.)

### 3. Emphasize Observation Over Judgment

✅ **Good**:
- "평가가 아니라 관찰이에요." (It's observation, not evaluation.)
- "어땠어요?" (How was it?)
- "생각보다 쉬웠어요" (It was easier than expected.)

❌ **Avoid**:
- "잘했나요?" (Did you do well?)
- "실패했나요?" (Did you fail?)
- "평점을 매겨주세요." (Rate yourself.)

### 4. Use Action-Oriented Verbs

✅ **Good**:
- "시작해요" (Start)
- "해볼게요" (Try it)
- "쪼개기" (Break down)
- "찍기" (Place/record)

❌ **Avoid**:
- "진행" (Progress)
- "수행" (Perform)
- "기록하다" (Log/record — too formal)

## Specific Screens & Phrases

### Onboarding

**Intro Slides**:
- "시작하지 못하는 건 게으른 게 아니에요." (Not starting isn't laziness.)
- "완료하지 않아도 괜찮아요. 시작했다는 것만 기록해요." (It's okay to not finish. We only record that you started.)
- "시작할 때마다 점 하나가 찍혀요." (A dot gets placed each time you start.)

**Milestone Setup**:
- "내 목표를 설정해봐요." (Let's set up your goals.)
- "마일스톤을 추가하면 점과 함께 기록돼요." (Milestones get recorded with your dots.)
- "최대 5개." (Max 5.)
- "마일스톤을 추가해봐요." (Add a milestone.)
- "아직 마일스톤이 없어요." (No milestones yet.)

---

### Mode A (Minimum Action)

**Step 1 - Input**:
- "지금 뭐가 걸려있어요?" (What's weighing on you right now?)
- "부담 없이 툭 적어요. 잘 쓰지 않아도 돼요." (Jot it down casually. No need to write well.)
- "여러 개 적고, 하나를 골라봐요." (Write multiple, pick one.)
- "할 일을 추가해봐요." (Add a task.)

**Step 2 - Suggestion**:
- "그럼 오늘은 이것만 해볼까요?" (So how about just this today?)
- "완벽하지 않아도 괜찮아요." (Imperfection is fine.)
- "더 작게 쪼개기" (Break it down smaller.)

**Step 3 - Timer**:
- "준비됐으면 시작해요." (When you're ready, start.)
- "타이머를 설정하면 더 가볍게 시작할 수 있어요." (Setting a timer makes it easier to start.)

**Step 4 - Done**:
- "하고 있는 중이에요." (You're doing it.)
- "점 찍기" (Place a dot)

**Alternative**:
- "오늘은 그냥 쉴게요." (I'll just rest today.)
- "쉬기로 했어요." (I decided to rest.)

---

### Mode B (Step-by-Step)

**Step 1 - Goal Input**:
- "오늘 뭘 해볼 거예요?" (What will you do today?)
- "크게 쓰셔도, 작게 쓰셔도 괜찮아요." (Big or small, it's all okay.)
- "여러 개 적고, 하나를 골라봐요." (Write multiple, pick one.)
- "할 일을 추가해봐요." (Add a goal.)

**Step 2 - Steps Breakdown**:
- "이렇게 나눠볼게요." (Let's break it down this way.)
- "수정해도 괜찮아요." (Feel free to edit.)
- "수정할게요." (I'll edit.)
- "완료." (Done editing.)

**Step 3 - Execution**:
- "[n]단계 / [total]단계" ([n] step / [total] steps)
- "완료, 다음 단계로 →" (Done, next step →)
- "모두 완료했어요." (I've done all steps.)
- "오늘은 여기까지." (I'll stop here for today.)

**Step 4 - Reflection**:
- "어땠어요?" (How was it?)
- "평가가 아니라 관찰이에요." (It's observation, not evaluation.)

**Reflection Options**:
- "생각보다 쉬웠어요." (It was easier than expected.)
- "생각보다 어려웠어요." (It was harder than expected.)
- "오래 걸렸어요." (It took a long time.)
- "그냥 그랬어요." (It was just okay.)

**Note Prompt**:
- "기억하고 싶은 게 있으면 적어요. (선택)" (Write anything you want to remember. (optional))

## Writing Dos & Don'ts

### ✅ DO

- Use short, simple sentences (8-12 words max)
- Use action verbs in imperative (해봐요, 적어요, 시작해요)
- Use closing '요' for warmth (해요, 괜찮아요, 있어요)
- Use emoji sparingly (💧 for dot only)
- Use informal/conversational Korean (반말 softened with 요)
- Include white space in UI text
- Use line breaks for readability

### ❌ DON'T

- Don't use formal/polite Korean (합니다, 입니다, 하십시오)
- Don't use business/corporate language (진행, 수행, 현황)
- Don't assume users know app terminology (explain gently)
- Don't use emojis in buttons/labels (only in special places)
- Don't use ALL CAPS
- Don't use passive voice (피동형 피하기)
- Don't make text longer than 15 words per line

## Korean Language Notes

### Grammar

- Use present tense (-어요, -해요) for warmth and immediacy
- Avoid past tense (었어요) unless describing reflection
- Use imperative softened with 요 (해봐요, 적어요, 골라봐요)
- Avoid 존댓말 (formal) — use 반말 with 요 for friendliness

### Particles

- 가/이 (subject): 점이 찍혀요 (a dot gets placed)
- 을/를 (object): 마일스톤을 추가하면 (if you add a milestone)
- 는 (topic): 타이머는 선택이에요 (timer is optional)

### Informal Honorifics

- -세요 is too formal; use -요 instead
- 우리 / 함께 (we/together) → builds connection
- 너 / 당신 → avoid; use imperative verbs instead

---

## Translation Tips

- Don't just translate English → Korean
- Write naturally in Korean first
- Test phrases with native Korean speakers
- Short, punchy Korean is warmer than long sentences
- Korean loves patterns & rhythm (use them for memorability)
